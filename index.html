ABOUT

[ I'm a photographer based in Cologne, working mostly on 35mm film. My work moves across landscapes, everyday moments and portraits.

I'm currently in a phase of figuring out my own voice, moving away from just shooting everywhere and toward more intentional, editorial and still life work, along with portraits that lean more artsy.

I'm continuing to explore where my photography wants to go next, including a longtime dream of shooting concerts and possibly working in medium format.
